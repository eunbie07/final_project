=== SPATIAL POSITIONING PATTERNS ===

Successful Center Placement Indicators:
- Relative position (0.45-0.55, 0.45-0.55) = True center zone
- Center distance < 0.1 = Perfect center
- Center distance < 0.2 = Near center (acceptable)
- Center distance > 0.3 = Edge placement

Room Size Categories:
Small Room: <15㎡ - furniture should be 40-60% from walls
Medium Room: 15-25㎡ - furniture can be exactly centered
Large Room: >25㎡ - multiple furniture pieces, zoned layouts

=== STYLE-SPECIFIC LAYOUT PRINCIPLES ===

Industrial Style:
- Prefers unconventional furniture placement
- Center placement works well with minimalist approach
- Raw materials complement unusual positioning

Scandinavian Style:
- Natural light important - avoid blocking windows
- Clean lines support center placement
- Light wood tones work with open floor space

Bohemian Style:
- Layered textures, but model tends toward wall placement
- Requires stronger prompting for center positioning

Cozy Style:
- Model strongly biased toward wall-adjacent placement
- Needs explicit "unconventional" positioning instructions

=== PROMPT OPTIMIZATION LESSONS ===

Effective Phrases for Center Placement:
- "EXACTLY at coordinates X=Xcm, Z=Zcm"  
- "THIS IS PERFECT CENTER placement"
- "Modern artistic installation style"
- "Unconventional furniture arrangement"
- "NOT typical bedroom layout"

Ineffective Approaches:
- Vague positioning like "center of room"
- Relying on style alone without explicit coordinates
- Not countering model's conventional layout bias

=== TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS ===

Coordinate System:
- Origin (0,0) at front-left corner of room
- X-axis: left to right (width)
- Z-axis: front to back (depth) 
- Y-axis: floor to ceiling (height)

Precision Requirements:
- Coordinates specified to 0.1cm accuracy
- Relative positions calculated to 0.001 precision
- Center distance measured as Euclidean distance from (0.5, 0.5)

Success Metrics:
- Coordinate accuracy: ±10cm acceptable, ±5cm preferred
- Furniture count accuracy: Must match exactly
- Style consistency: Visual elements match requested style
- Overall user satisfaction: 4.0/5.0+ for knowledge base inclusion
